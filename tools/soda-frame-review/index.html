Groups

To add a group, open Frame editor and stage at least two exact frames with 1, then press M. You can also choose New group (yellow staging) beside a frame and stage more exact frames. Existing groups keep their identity and membership when configured here.

Set a unique positive group number and color, then choose Save group settings. Editing either field alone never writes. In a group, choose an existing destination and press Move, or press Remove from group to correct one exact membership. Groups without a saved color use the neutral default until you save settings.

Move an exact member to recoverable Trash, or restore it here, without removing its group membership, category or saved decision. This does not delete original files. Move/Remove membership is separate.

Saga map is an independent category. Check or uncheck it and press Save category for each exact frame or group. Changing the checkbox alone never saves. A group category labels only its reference, not its members or Keep decisions. Moving or removing a member preserves its direct frame category.
